About the event

The NeuroTech Summit brings together the region's neuroscience researchers, machine-learning engineers, clinicians, regulators and investors for three days of keynotes, hands-on BCI workshops and clinical demonstrations. The 2026 edition focuses on translating research into deployable neurohealth care.
